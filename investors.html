Investment Process

How to Invest in Dhanam

Investing in Dhanam NCDs is simple and can be done through our branches or online. Follow these steps to get started.

1

Contact Our Investor Relations Team

Reach out to us via phone at +91 73050 22272, WhatsApp, email, or visit your nearest Dhanam branch. Our team will walk you through all available NCD schemes and help you choose the best fit.

2

Select Your Scheme & Tenure

Choose from our four NCD schemes — Doubling, Annual, Cumulative, or Monthly — based on your financial goals. Pick a tenure from 13 to 60 months. Our team will provide a detailed illustration of your expected returns.

3

Submit KYC & Demat Documents

Provide your identity proof, address proof, PAN card, bank details, and Demat account details (DP ID & Client ID). NCDs are allotted in demat form, so an active Demat account is mandatory.

4

Transfer Funds & Sign Agreement

Transfer the investment amount via NEFT/RTGS or cheque. Review and sign the NCD subscription agreement. All terms, interest rates, and maturity details are clearly outlined before you sign.

5

NCDs Credited to Your Demat Account

Once your application is processed and funds received, your NCDs will be allotted and credited directly to your Demat account. You'll receive a confirmation with your NCD certificate details, ISIN number, and maturity schedule.

6

Receive Interest & Maturity Payout

Interest payouts (monthly/annual/cumulative) and principal redemption at maturity are credited directly to your registered bank account via NEFT. You'll be notified ahead of every payout.

Why Demat Form?

  • Secure & Paperless — No risk of physical certificates being lost, stolen, or damaged
  • Easy Tracking — View your NCD holdings anytime through your Demat account statement
  • Transferable — NCDs held in demat can be transferred to another demat account if needed
  • Regulatory Compliant — Demat allotment is mandated by RBI and SEBI for investor protection

Don't have a Demat account?

You can open a Demat account with any SEBI-registered depository participant (DP) such as Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, ICICI Direct, HDFC Securities, etc. The process is fully online and typically completed in 1–2 days.

Our team can guide you through the process. Contact us for assistance.
