Tax Considerations

Tax Implications

Interest earned on NCDs is taxable income as per your income tax slab. Consult your tax advisor for optimal planning.

  • Interest Income: Taxable as per your applicable income tax slab
  • TDS: Tax Deducted at Source may apply at 10-20% depending on investment amount and eligibility
  • Cumulative NCDs: Interest accrues and is taxed at maturity
  • Capital Gains: No capital gains if held to maturity (principal repaid in full)

Note: For accurate tax planning, please consult your chartered accountant or tax advisor.
