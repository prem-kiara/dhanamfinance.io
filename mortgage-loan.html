Eligibility Criteria

Who Can Apply?

Salaried Borrowers

  • Age: 21 to 65 years
  • Minimum Monthly Income: ₹20,000
  • CIBIL Score: 650 and above
  • Employment Stability: Minimum 2 years in current employment

Self-Employed Borrowers

  • Age: 25 to 70 years
  • Business Vintage: Minimum 3 years
  • CIBIL Score: 650 and above
  • Net Worth: Positive and verified
Eligible Properties

Property Types Accepted

Accepted Properties

  • Freehold residential properties
  • Clear-title vacant land (freehold)
  • Self-occupied residential units

NOT Accepted

  • Commercial properties
  • Industrial properties
  • Leasehold properties
Documentation

Documents Required

Keep these ready for a smooth application process.

Personal KYC

  • Aadhar / Pan / Passport
  • Address proof
  • Bank statements (6 months)
  • Photo ID

Income Proof

  • Salary slips (3 months)
  • IT returns (2 years)
  • Employment letter
  • Financial statements (self-employed)

Property Documents

  • Sale deed / Land records
  • Property tax receipt
  • Registered layout plan
  • Legal opinion
