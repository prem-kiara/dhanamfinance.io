Our Story

From a single branch in Coimbatore to branches across South India, Dhanam's journey is built on trust, innovation, and a commitment to financial inclusion.

Who We Are

Built on Trust, Driven by Purpose

Founded in 2016 in Coimbatore, Dhanam Investment and Finance Pvt. Ltd. was born from a vision to bring formal financial services to underserved communities. Starting with microfinance and MFI lending, we recognized the potential to serve rural farmers, small business owners, and working families who faced barriers to accessing credit from traditional banking systems.

Over the years, we've expanded from microfinance into gold loans, mortgage lending, and SME finance — and in 2026, we're launching Premium Private Lockers, offering secure, state-of-the-art vault storage for valuables.

The name "Dhanam" holds deep meaning, derived from Sanskrit and Tamil words meaning "wealth." But for us, true wealth isn't just about money — it's about empowering communities, enabling dreams, and fostering progress.

Our Growth

From One Branch to Fifty-Seven

We started with microfinance and MFI lending, serving rural farmers and entrepreneurs. In 2024, we launched Loan Against Property (LAP/mortgage) services, in 2025 we expanded into gold loans, and in 2026 we're introducing Premium Private Lockers — bringing secure vault storage to our customers. Throughout this evolution, we've stayed true to our core mission: fair lending, transparent dealings, and accessible credit for all.

Today, a decade later, Dhanam stands as a testament to what's possible when trust meets innovation. We've served over 5,00,000 customers and disbursed more than ₹1000 crores — yet our founding principles remain unchanged.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

2016

Founded in Coimbatore

Dhanam opens its first branch in Coimbatore with a focus on microfinance and MFI lending. Applied for RBI NBFC license.

2017

RBI NBFC License Received

Received RBI NBFC license, marking our official status as a non-banking financial company. Expanded microfinance operations across Tamil Nadu.

2018

Raised NCD Book Worth ₹45+ Cr

Successfully raised an NCD book worth ₹45+ crore, strengthening our capital base and enabling expanded lending operations across Tamil Nadu.

2019

Expanded Reach

Expanded to 15+ branches across Tamil Nadu, serving rural and semi-urban communities with microfinance solutions.

2021

Major Milestone Reached

Crossed ₹500 crore in loan disbursals with 30+ branches operating across Tamil Nadu.

2024

LAP (Mortgage) Launch

Launched Loan Against Property (LAP/mortgage) services, diversifying our product portfolio and reaching more customers.

2025

Gold Loan Launch

Launched Gold Loan services, expanding our offerings. Now operating across branches in South India.

2026

A Decade Strong & Premium Private Lockers

Celebrating 10 years with branches across South India and ₹1000 crore+ disbursed. Launched Premium Private Lockers — secure, state-of-the-art vault storage for valuables — expanding beyond lending into comprehensive wealth protection.
