Our Story

From a single branch in Coimbatore to 57 branches across Tamil Nadu, Dhanam's journey is built on trust, innovation, and a commitment to financial inclusion.

Who We Are

Built on Trust, Driven by Purpose

Founded in 2010 in Coimbatore, Dhanam Investment and Finance Pvt. Ltd. was born from a simple yet powerful vision: to bridge the financial gap left by traditional banking systems. Our visionary leaders recognized that everyday Indians—farmers, small business owners, and working families—were being overlooked by conventional banks.

The name "Dhanam" holds deep meaning, derived from Sanskrit and Tamil words meaning "wealth." But for us, true wealth isn't just about money—it's about empowering communities, enabling dreams, and fostering progress.

Our Growth

From One Branch to Fifty-Seven

We started with gold loans, a product close to the hearts of Indian families. From there, we expanded into mortgage lending and SME financing, always staying true to our core mission: fair lending, transparent dealings, and accessible credit for all.

Today, sixteen years later, Dhanam stands as a testament to what's possible when trust meets innovation. We've served over 5,00,000 customers and disbursed more than ₹1000 crores — yet our founding principles remain unchanged.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

2010

Founded in Coimbatore

Dhanam opens its first branch in Coimbatore with a focus on gold loans and financial inclusion.

2012

Expansion Begins

Gold loan operations expand across Tamil Nadu, reaching 5 branches and serving more families.

2015

Mortgage Lending Launch

Launch of Loan Against Property (LAP) services, expanding our product portfolio to 15 branches.

2018

Major Milestone Reached

Cross ₹500 crore in loan disbursals with 30+ branches operating across Tamil Nadu.

2020

SME & Digital Growth

Launch of SME financing and digital transformation initiatives to better serve our customers.

2023

Investor Growth

Reach 50+ branches and launch NCD offerings, attracting institutional and retail investors.

2026

A Decade & More

57 branches, ₹1000 crore+ disbursed, serving 5,00,000+ customers with trust and excellence.
