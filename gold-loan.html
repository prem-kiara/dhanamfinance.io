Key Features

What You Get

24 Hour Disbursal

Get cash in your account within 24 hours of approval. No waiting. No delays.

Fully Insured

Gold stored in tamper-proof vaults with comprehensive insurance covering theft, burglary, fire, and flood.

No Pledging Fee

We don't charge pledging fees. What you see is what you pay.

Flexible Repayment

Bullet repayment (max 12 months) or EMI-based. Pay via NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, cheque, or NACH auto-debit.

Easy Top-ups

Add more gold to your loan for additional funds. Quick approvals.

Redemption Anytime

Redeem your gold whenever you want. No lock-in period. Your gold, your rules.

Transparency & Security

Policy Protections

Key Fact Statement

We provide a detailed Key Fact Statement before disbursement with all terms, interest rates, charges, and repayment schedules disclosed upfront. No surprises.

Auction Protection

If your loan defaults, we conduct public auctions with notice in regional and national newspapers. Reserve price minimum 90% of market value. Surplus returned within 7 working days.

Who Can Apply

Simple Eligibility

  • Age: Minimum 18 years
  • Gold: All gold jewelry and ornaments (purity assessed by certified assayers)
  • Documents: ID proof (Aadhar, passport, driving license) and address proof. PAN mandatory for loans above Rs 5 lakh
  • Valuation: Gold valuation by certified assayers in your presence with full transparency
