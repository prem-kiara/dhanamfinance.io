Governance & Compliance

Our Policies

Transparency and regulatory compliance are at the heart of everything we do. Every policy is available for your review.

Regulatory Policies

Committed to Transparency

As an RBI-licensed NBFC, Dhanam maintains comprehensive policies that govern our operations, protect our customers, and ensure regulatory compliance. We believe in complete transparency — every policy is available for your review.

✍

Code of Conduct

Dhanam's Code of Conduct establishes the ethical standards and professional behaviour expected of all employees, directors, and stakeholders. It covers integrity in dealings, confidentiality of customer information, prevention of conflicts of interest, and commitment to fair business practices.

⚖

Fair Practice Code

In compliance with RBI guidelines, our Fair Practice Code outlines the principles of fair lending, transparent communication, and customer rights. It covers loan application processes, terms disclosure, non-discriminatory practices, recovery procedures, and the right to privacy. All lending decisions are made on objective criteria.

◆

Gold Loan Policy

Our Gold Loan Policy details the terms and conditions for gold-backed lending including eligible gold purity standards, loan-to-value ratios, interest rate structure, auction procedures, safe custody of pledged ornaments, and insurance coverage. All gold valuations are conducted transparently in the customer's presence.

%

Interest Rate Policy

Dhanam's Interest Rate Policy outlines how lending rates are determined based on cost of funds, risk assessment, regulatory guidelines, and market conditions. The policy ensures rates are fair, transparent, and non-discriminatory. Interest is calculated on a reducing balance basis, and all applicable charges are disclosed upfront.

LP

Loan Pricing Policy

Our Loan Pricing Policy establishes the framework for determining processing fees, prepayment charges, late payment penalties, and other service charges across all loan products. All pricing is benchmarked against market standards and disclosed to customers before loan sanction.

PP

Privacy Policy

Dhanam is committed to protecting customer data. Our Privacy Policy details how personal and financial information is collected, used, stored, and protected. We employ industry-standard security measures and never share customer information with third parties without consent, except as required by law.

VM

Vigil Mechanism / Whistle Blower Policy

In line with corporate governance best practices, Dhanam maintains a Vigil Mechanism that enables employees and stakeholders to report concerns about unethical behaviour, fraud, or violations of company policy. The mechanism ensures confidentiality and protection against retaliation for whistle blowers.

TC

Terms & Conditions

General terms and conditions governing the use of Dhanam's services, including loan agreements, repayment obligations, default consequences, and dispute resolution mechanisms. All terms are explained clearly at the time of loan sanction.
